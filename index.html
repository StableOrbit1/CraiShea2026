The Details

Tournament Information

CraiShea 2026 is the 3rd Annual Season Ending Birthday Celebration for Craig & Shea . . . not an overly creative name.

Entry is $50, with all of that going back to the participants, based on their performance.

Date

Saturday, August 15th, 2026

Venue

The Nest at Friday Harbour

The Rules

  • Play your own ball.
  • Can move out of divots in the fairway and footprints in the traps.
  • The Breakfast Ball: Available on hole one, but if you elect to hit a second ball, you're committed to it.
  • NEW: The Birthday Ball. You can drop and replay one shot in your round, from anywhere except the green. Swings, no putts.
